Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Collection data

Bottle Vault stores your collection in its private storage on your iPhone. This includes bottle details, purchase prices and dates, estimated values, ratings, notes, shelves, wishlist items, and photos you add. Saved barcodes and product details from earlier versions can also remain on the device.

Bottle Vault requires no account and does not upload or sync your collection to a server. Barcode scanning and online lookup are disabled in this version. The app uses no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple may provide app diagnostics and usage statistics according to your device settings.

Camera and photos

Bottle Vault uses the camera when you choose Take Photo. Apple's photo picker gives the app access to the photo you select. The app re-encodes the photo for its collection without the original location metadata, and background removal runs on your iPhone. If you choose to save a collection image to Photos, Bottle Vault requests permission to add the image.

Backups and sharing

Your collection and in-app safety backups can be included in iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your device settings. Bottle Vault does not receive those backups.

When you export a spreadsheet, make a backup, or share a collection image, you choose where it goes. The recipient or service you choose handles its copy. Backup files can include photos, prices and notes and are not encrypted by Bottle Vault. Collection images omit prices, estimated values and notes by default. Copying a collection image uses the system clipboard, which can be shared across your Apple devices through Universal Clipboard.

Deleting data

You can remove individual items or use More → Delete All Data to remove your collection, in-app safety backups and temporary exports. If a backup or temporary file cannot be removed, the app alerts you. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Copies in device backups, Photos or other destinations you exported or shared to are managed separately.

Website and email

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. If you email Bottle Vault, we receive your email address and anything you include, and use that information to respond to your request.
